## 0. Why this file exists

Two content-quality problems this file solves:

1. **Term drift** — the same concept written three different ways across posts erodes the brand's authority. Rule: always use the "{{BRAND_NAME}} standard" term below, never an alternate, even when multiple valid terms exist.
2. **Re-researching facts** — a historical/cultural claim verified once for a post should never need re-verification. Verified facts land in Section 6 with their source.
